---

Personal portfolio website. Creative Designer & Motion Designer. Goal: showcase
work and experience; the main CTA redirects to Telegram DMs.

**Style** — minimal, premium, subtle glassmorphism. Light theme, soft neutral
palette, 2–3 modern fonts that pair well, smooth scrolling animations. Every
section animates both entering and leaving the viewport, combining opacity,
blur, and subtle movement.

**Hero** — portrait photo; large modern typographic treatment of first and last
name; clean premium layout with plenty of whitespace; smooth entrance
animations.

**Inspiration** — derekherman.co and mark.skrypka.co. Not a copy; the same
level of quality, elegance and interaction.

**Structure** — Hero, Velar Studio, Career, Portfolio (vertical 9:16 images and
videos), CTA, Footer.

**Velar Studio** — the creative agency he is building. Specialises in:
high-converting static advertising creatives; motion creatives; AI-assisted
advertising creatives; short-form video ads; social media content; business
marketing creatives.
